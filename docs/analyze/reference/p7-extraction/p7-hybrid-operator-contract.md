# P7 hybrid operator contract (implementation draft)

Status: unverified; this contract does not confer physical acceptance. CPU/F64, SI units; phasors use exp(+jωt). Existing coupled equations are D i + jω q = s and R i + jω L i - Dᵀ v = 0, q = C v. D is the signed divergence, positive outward; s is injection into the conductor. Port current is positive into its positive terminal and negative at its negative terminal; port voltage is v+ - v-. DC is solved as its own limit, never by division by ω.

## Coordinates and interfaces

A planar prism has a triangular XY footprint and finite z interval in metres. Constant current basis densities J_k in m^-2 multiply coefficients i_k in A; charge densities ρ_k in m^-3 multiply q_k in C. Their volume integrals define their actual normalizations; no implicit unit normalization is assumed. Magnetic Gram Lkl = μ0/(4π) ∫∫ J_k(x)·J_l(y)/|x-y| dx dy [H]; electric potential-coefficient Gram Pkl = 1/(4πε) ∫∫ ρ_k(x)ρ_l(y)/|x-y| dx dy [1/F]. P maps charges to dual potentials; it is not the capacitance C used by the existing coupled model. Elimination/inversion and constraints must be explicit before integration into that model. Resistance integrates J_k·J_l/σ [Ω].

For potential and current projection matrices V and I, use D_r = Vᵀ D I and dual injection s_r = Vᵀ s. Interface current orientations are outward from each domain; enforce sum of opposing fluxes zero and equality of projected potentials by transpose-paired constraints, preserving vᵀs and power. No finite contact is created by proximity. Every disconnected conductor retains its own constant potential mode and charge balance; gauge fixes only the common additive nullspace where the chosen field formulation has one. It must not short islands or pin their physical potentials. Floating island continuity is Σ(Di+jωq-s)=0 per component, including capacitive current; impose prescribed net charge when physically specified. Existing foil_reduced and island_response retain component modes and must remain the reference for these constraints.

## Ownership and computation

One disjoint physical copper support has exactly one owner (planar, local volume, or section); split or exclude overlapping pads/barrels before assembly. A single hybrid assembler owns every unordered field pair and adds its transpose. planar_operator owns planar–planar Gram via prism::volume_integral/axial_magnetic::constant_pair; hybrid_section_magnetic and its volume/tetra kernels own section–local cross terms until a compact cross API exists. The assembler must explicitly supply planar–volume and section–planar blocks; missing blocks refuse the coupled route. Self and touching/near pairs use singular-aware prism kernels, never smooth far-only quadrature. Distinct overlapping supports refuse until partitioned. No full section RLGC ground or loss may be stamped atop fields/resistance represented in the global system. Existing grouped_gram uses tetra fallback for touching sections and is not itself a compact full-board operator.

The automatic compact path now accepts section-derived current predictors in its existing physical current coordinates. `mixed_conductor::uniform_section_profiles` supplies conforming axial profiles, and `planar_section_profiles` samples a bounded XY cross-section on physical foil supports. Least-squares restriction maps the initial profiles to compact coordinates. `solve_compact_adaptive` orthogonalizes them in the physical resistive metric and uses the same common electric/magnetic actions and original-coordinate residual enrichment as other currents. Sampling defines a candidate span only. It does not certify longitudinal homogeneity or transfer an independent section impedance, loss matrix or ground stamp. This path has no separately owned section copper and requires no extra section–planar field block.

## Adaptive return-domain observation

`board-channel-plan` supplies an in-memory expansion callback for automatic scopes. `runAutoFoil` doubles the search margin through scales 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128, sharing the original wall and memory limits. Each candidate closes over complete intersecting copper islands, all layers, plated barrels and declared component contacts. Authored domain polygons retain their explicit scope.

`geometry.returnBoundaryMm` defines a six-face XYZ observation box, with world XY coordinates and stack-origin Z in mm. The solved field extends beyond every face. All physical electrode nodes, including package contacts, must lie strictly inside the box. Prescribed terminal injections therefore never intersect the observed boundary. The returned DSL plan uses the actual expanded domain and retained sources.

For each frequency and matched Norton drive, the observed unresolved-current bound is

`F = sum_faces integral |J_complex dot n| dA + omega * sum_faces integral |D_complex dot n| dA`.

`I = |I_net at the driven physical input port|`, `rho = F / max(I, 1e-12 A)`.

Admission requires `I > 1e-12 A` and `rho < 0.01` for all requested columns and computed frequency-audit columns. The 1 pA floor is fixed before solving. Internal package currents and larger currents at other ports do not enter the denominator. The consumer recomputes the sum, ratio and pass flag and checks the driven-port normalization against S and the original references. Exceeding the threshold expands the domain. Budget exhaustion, invalid evidence or eight unresolved candidates produces a refusal with available iteration history.

`return_boundary::conductive` integrates physical P0 current density over clipped tetrahedron sections. `SpatialFieldAction::cut_displacement_flux` reconstructs its prepared prism field and integrates a conservative complex-normal-field magnitude bound over the internal cuts. It uses actual dielectric permittivity and accounts separately for zero-displacement copper coverage. Shared coplanar interfaces have one interior-side owner, and complete face coverage is required. The calculation uses a compensated physical-potential lift with one global gauge and retains inter-island voltage differences. It never substitutes free-row residuals or outer natural-boundary reactions for missing return flux.

The evidence policy is `internal-guard-flux-v1`. This certifies the numerical guard-current criterion in the computed domain. Independent field discretization, exterior-domain sensitivity and physical accuracy retain their existing unverified status until acceptance.

## Applicability and checks

The initial constant-through-thickness basis is experimental. For conductivity σ, permeability μ, frequency f, δ = 1/sqrt(π f μ σ); at DC δ is infinite. Require t/δ <= 0.2 for this initial approximation, otherwise enrich thickness/current modes or escalate to local volume; this conservative screening is not an accuracy proof. Nearby conductors, edges, gaps and strong field gradients require independent proximity/refinement evidence even when this test passes. Homogeneous ε is required by the initial Coulomb kernel; layered dielectric must use a validated layered Green operator or refuse. Geometric ownership, coincident supports, invalid material, non-finite values and budget/deadline violations refuse.

For each pair evaluate orders n and n+4, n in 8..60; require |K(n+4)-K(n)| <= 1e-8 max(|K(n+4)|, scale_floor). This is an estimator, not certified physical error. Preserve existing stricter physical gates and check KCL/KVL separately from approximation error. Check reciprocity, nonnegative energy/passivity, component charge, two independent drives and interface power. Enrich current/interface modes when residual or observable variation exceeds the existing request accuracy budget; cap by request memory/deadline/mode budget, and escalate to local volume or return unverified/refused if no converged representation fits. Compare independent short volume reference and refinement, including self, touching, slit and floating island. A residual in the reduced equations alone is not physical acceptance.

API (experimental): `planar_operator::gram(supports, modes, order, epsilon, budget, limit)` produces separate P and L matrices on compact basis coefficients. `Prism` geometry is validated by `Prism::new`; support owns density arrays per mode. The initial API does not claim dielectric layers, contacts, conductivity, solver, or runtime coverage. Required controls: self/near convergence, symmetry and two-support cross term, zero density, invalid dielectric and duplicate/overlap refusal, budget and deadline refusal, plus a small independent volume comparison before admission.

Layered implementation route: `planar_layered::capacitance(input, trace, budget)` invokes `layered_capacitance::extract_traced` and `Solution::energy_capacitance_f`. It solves the explicitly meshed layered dielectric, retaining only the requested trace-coordinate C as its output. This is a bounded intermediate integration, not a proof of compact field action: the field solve still materializes its own dielectric mesh and bases. The trace adapter must cover every boundary node and preserve partition of unity and component constants; mesh/exterior refinement and independent references remain required. The homogeneous prism Gram takes independent charge and current mode counts; exact zero supports/modes are skipped, but its density storage is currently dense.

## Direct near-integral reductions

Composition forms the candidate span from both each block's physical charge modes and its certified structural freedoms before applying weld/current constraints. A package's zero-energy local gauge can become part of a physical conserved-charge mode after joining capacitive copper. The separately certified global structural gauge is retained; C and its common-mode energy are unchanged. Orthogonalization and intersection use the existing two-pass/SVD tolerances. The corrected coupled DC model preserves total initial charge rather than pinning a physical potential or enforcing separate charge on islands connected by a component.

Adaptive samples include Hermitian matched-drive loss Grams G_ij=conj(q_i)^T R q_j for copper and R_pkg*conj(I_pkg,i)*I_pkg,j for each package. Their sum must match D*(I-S^H*S)*D, D_j=sqrt(R_j)/2. Diagonals retain the original per-drive Joule loss. Only reduced coefficients are retained; output admission includes JSON object allocation and owner identities before forming the matrices. Consumer checks Hermitian PSD, finite dimensions, ownership, package-current consistency, diagonals and total complex wave power. Modal Norton drive weights are Q_ji*sqrt(R_mode,j/R_i), so actual copper/package modal losses include cross terms. Projection rejects material negative energy and uses only floating-point scale/underflow tolerance for rounding; the existing absolute wave-power comparison floor does not hide a negative weak loss Gram.

`P7_HYBRID_REGIME_AUDIT=1` requests a bounded discrete-model control after adaptive training. It evaluates matched DC and a sinusoidal probe, initializes the same P6 descriptor from the AC periodic state and compares two transient refinements with unchanged residual, energy and source-front guards. Probe<=upper/20 preserves the existing temporal source screen. The probe can lie below the original exact grid: this checks algebraic model consistency only, and neither widens authored component/material applicability nor grants physical DC/time-band acceptance or P8/P11 readiness. Requested evidence is cache-isolated and validated; a failed requested control is refused.

Existing differential DSL targets supply explicit positive/negative leg pairings through the physical planner; no name-based guessing or reference-plane connection is introduced. For equal real reference R in a pair, orthogonal power-wave rows are (1,-1)/sqrt(2) and (1,1)/sqrt(2), with differential reference 2R and common reference R/2. Unpaired ports retain their basis. `mixedMode` publishes Q*S*Q^T with source-port/pair metadata. For a matched 1 A modal Norton drive j, total accepted power is R_mode,j/4 times (1-sum_i |S_mode,ij|²), using the driven column. Only roundoff-sized negative values are bounded/clipped; material negative power refuses. Separate modal copper/package losses use the checked full loss Grams described above when available. Diagonal-only samples do not determine modal losses. Original physical-port network replay is preserved.

Mixed response/package output is reserved inside the shared mixed producer before map/model construction. Direct mesh-building callers use a separate mesh budget and pass the original producer budget into that admission, so the same reserve is deducted once. Reports restore only actually reserved amounts. The board endpoint's separate serialization reserve stays outside the mixed working budget. Planned bytes and reported memory must remain within the API producer budget; an otherwise completed solve does not bypass this check.

`boardFoilPortObservations` recovers differential port quantities for a 1 A RMS matched Norton drive j: a_j=sqrt(R_j)/2, V_i=sqrt(R_i)*a_j*(delta_ij+S_ij), I_i=delta_ij-V_i/R_i. R_i is the wave reference, not the DUT resistance. Entering currents map to the actual positive physical contact and their negatives to its paired negative contact; aggregation uses exactly sourcePath plus layer, with no inferred galvanic ground. Native electrode names are distinct from terminal IDs. Native copper/package losses remain separate. Terminal return currents come from the port response. For the compact producer, native `spatialRecovery` separately lifts the solved current coordinates to foil and tetrahedron supports and publishes current density and Joule loss. Physical validity remains conditional.

The distinct `hybrid-planar-v3` profiled policy requires `automatic-hybrid-v1`. Its implementation input envelope is 1 kHz..10 GHz, matching the P7 upper exclusion boundary rather than granting physical applicability. The existing native electrical-size screen k*diameter*sqrt(epsilon_r)<=0.1, finite pad/contact checks, δ/5 planar spacing, δ/3 barrel spacing and all allocation/accuracy checks remain mandatory. Legacy operators keep their 10/100 kHz limits, and profile upper bounds must obey the explicitly selected operator. The common mesh metadata divisor remains 3; the hybrid policy additionally validates every planar layer at divisor 5. Wider input admission alone proves no RF result or accuracy.

`frequencyAudit.adaptiveResponse` exposes sorted exact baseline and successfully validated supplemental physical responses, including losses/power and independent holdout outcomes. Shared-mesh identity uses stable compact coordinate/operator fields and physical ownership, excluding frequency-dependent quadrature statistics. A later refused batch does not erase earlier valid points. Original network replay remains on the original producer grid; supplemental points and sensitivity tests do not establish continuous-band or independent physical acceptance.

Adaptive mixed training now seeds thickness profiles only on contiguous foil groups spanning a complete physical layer triangle with uniform conductivity. For thickness t and unit-L2 real profile u_k(z), a DC current coordinate is multiplied by sqrt(t)*u_k on each slab, using the same current lift and incidence as the slab map. Up to four real nonuniform profiles per global DC seed enter the existing R-metric orthogonalization. Incomplete groups next to local volume are skipped. Hints are basis candidates: the coupled solve and full mixed-coordinate residual/enrichment still decide whether a response is usable; a hint is not separately asserted to satisfy KCL or prove local interface/RF accuracy. Descriptor, hint and group scratch bounds are admitted before harmonic construction, and retained peak accounting includes the larger profile plan.

Standalone exact-grid physical-port resources can expose S, Y or Z using the existing P6 frequency solver and its conditioning/passivity guards. With D=diag(sqrt(R)), Z=D(I+S)(I-S)^-1 D and Y=D^-1(I-S)(I+S)^-1 D^-1 where invertible. No common ground or network connection is introduced by this algebra. Physical pair order, producer frequencies and original wave references remain fixed; derived Y/Z resources retain the original S table as provenance and replay their own supplied Y/Z coefficients. Both coefficient tables count toward the pre-clone resource cap. This conversion inherits conditional physical validity and does not supply DC/time or band acceptance.

`automatic-hybrid-v1` explicitly selects the global compact adaptive mixed assembly, independently of the older full-basis switch. It is a distinct internal solver policy from `automatic-conductive-schur-v1`; its report must contain matching mixed coordinate counts and unverified accuracy, and cannot reuse Schur-solve evidence. The automatic TypeScript route always selects it. No board-name branch is used.

On resolved skin meshes above the full-layer thickness screen, `MixedMap` may partition a foil triangle by its actual extrusion z slabs. Each source tetrahedron retains exactly one owner, and lift/projection remain transpose-paired; resistance and incidence use the same original-cell congruence. The hybrid mesh target tightens current and field spacing to at most δ/5, and each actual foil support must still pass t_support/δ<=0.2 at the frozen profile upper frequency. This enriches tangential thickness currents while the depth potential restriction and transition halo remain approximations needing independent refinement; it does not establish pointwise three-dimensional continuity or RF acceptance. Existing implementation bands remain in force.

`P7_COMPACT_PL_THREADS=1..4` controls only pure planar/local near-kernel work. A bounded contiguous chunk retains serial pair order for accumulation/cache updates. Worker scratch and stacks are admitted before creating the pool; source geometry and quadrature tolerances are shared with serial evaluation. Cache identity includes the normalized count and hybrid generation.

The mixed magnetic action may use the same degree-four moment expansion for a geometrically separated pair classified as near by the tree. Admission requires every actual right-hand side and affected row to retain its cumulative absolute error at or below the original action goal. Absolute source-density envelopes preserve cancellation safety; positive products and sums round upward, including underflow to the smallest subnormal. Rejected or non-separated pairs use the original near kernel. Error allowances for initial columns derive from the existing resistive KVL scale at the requested upper frequency; no physical or residual threshold is enlarged. These bounds cover the moment tail only; near quadrature and physical approximation remain unverified.

The standalone `planar_skin::build` primitive supplies real, thickness-weighted orthonormal cell-average current profiles on caller-provided z intervals. With κ=(1+j)/δ, the even transport profile is κ[exp(-κz)+exp(-κ(t-z))]/[2(1-exp(-κt))], with integral one; the odd profile is κ[exp(-κ(t-z))-exp(-κz)]/(1-exp(-κt/2))², with integral zero. It retains transport coefficients and the positive resistive Gram integral J_i J_j/σ dz. Stable exponential differences avoid overflow. These are basis candidates, not separately stamped surface impedance: magnetic self energy and resistance each retain one owner. Adaptive training uses these profiles as described above. Conservative local-interface refinement and independent frequency validation remain required; per-support thickness screening and the existing implementation band limit stay active.

`planar_operator` now uses separate charge/current mode dimensions and sparse supports. Identical XY footprints use a triangle autocorrelation with both actual z intervals. Shared-vertex integration splits the two radial coordinates, integrates the common radius and z analytically, and leaves a three-dimensional positive quadrature.

For a common edge with barycentric variables u, v and d=lambda1-mu1, integrating the common-edge position gives length 1-max(v,u+d) when d>=0; d<0 follows by swapping triangles. Splitting at v=u+d and scaling r=max(v,u+d) gives radial weight r²(1-r). Its axial integral is analytic, leaving two-dimensional quadrature. All shape Jacobians retain 4*A_a*A_b. These are changes of integration variables on the same finite copper supports, not changes to the physical current/charge spaces. The same n/n+4 1e-8 estimator and endpoint-cancellation guards apply. Near quadrature remains estimated, not a certified physical-error bound.


## Production entry point and resource ownership

Automatic board AC and grouped SI use the compact joint planar and local-volume path through `solveBoardFoilChecked`. The legacy segmented and explicit local controls remain available for development and separate numerical APIs. Historical `budget` fields on the checked request remain compatible input; the hybrid solver does not use them for admission. Numerical domain, grid, convergence and overflow checks still apply. An explicit clipped AC scope is refused because the guarded hybrid requires closure of whole physical copper islands. The separate DC endpoint and explicit numerical APIs retain their own contracts.

The DSL host owns the scenario wall timeout through `runAnalysisDsl(..., { timeoutMs })`. The supervised command-line controls run the scenario directly under the Python process-tree supervisor, which owns elapsed time and sampled memory. Deployments requiring a process-memory cap must configure it in their external worker or process supervisor.
